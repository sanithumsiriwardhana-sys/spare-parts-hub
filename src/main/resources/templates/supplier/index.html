Procurement flow

The UC-05 path from approved restock requirement to inventory receipt.
1. Review requirement Open an approved restock suggestion from Inventory.
2. Compare suppliers Evaluate price, MOQ, lead time and valid supplier offers.
3. Issue PO Confirm supplier, quantity and price to create a Pending PO.
4. Track shipment Move a Pending order to Shipped after dispatch is confirmed.
5. Receive stock Record accepted units and serial numbers, then update inventory.
